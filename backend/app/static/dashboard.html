Install ANVEXA AI

Standalone App for Android, iOS, Windows & Mac

— OR MANUAL INSTALLATION —
🤖 Android (Chrome / Edge / Samsung)
  • 1. Tap the ⋮ (Menu) icon in your browser toolbar.
  • 2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  • 3. Tap Install to place ANVEXA AI on your home screen & app drawer.
🍏 iPhone & iPad (Safari)
  • 1. Open ANVEXA AI in Safari.
  • 2. Tap the Share button ⎋ in the bottom toolbar.
  • 3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" ⊞.
  • 4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
💻 PC & Mac (Chrome / Edge / Brave)
  • 1. Click the Install icon ⊕ on the right side of the URL address bar.
  • 2. Click Install to run ANVEXA AI as an independent desktop window app with taskbar pinning.
